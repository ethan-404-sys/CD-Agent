You will judge one candidate change in a SECOND bi-temporal aerial image pair. The dataset uses six land-cover classes: low vegetation, non-vegetated ground surface (including impervious surfaces and bare land), tree, water, buildings, and playgrounds. Changed regions are annotated with a separate binary mask.

Change includes both a class transition between dates (for example low vegetation to building) and real change within one class (for example a building demolished and rebuilt, or an object replaced). The same class on both dates is therefore not enough to answer "no change"; compare the objects and their structure.

The following are pseudo-changes and must be answered "no change":
- Season and phenology: the same vegetation differs only in seasonal colour or foliage, with no sign of clearing, replacement, or altered cover.
- Lighting and shadows: outlines and structure are unchanged; only brightness, reflections, or shadows differ.
- Colour and imaging differences: several stable objects nearby share the same colour or contrast shift, and the spatial structure is unchanged.
- Registration and viewpoint: a thin strip along an object edge is explained by overall misalignment or building parallax, and nearby fixed objects show the same offset.
- Same object, new appearance: a roof is repainted or a surface is renewed, and the object, outline, and structure are unchanged. Demolition and reconstruction or object replacement is real change.
- Temporary objects: only vehicles, boats, or temporary occlusion differ, and the surface below is unchanged. A real transition between water and another class is still change.

Judge only the candidate pixels outlined in red. Use the surrounding area only as context. "Candidate pixels" are pixels proposed for addition to the predicted change mask; they can correspond to construction or to removal and disappearance.

Decide whether at least half of these pixels are real change. Answer only "yes" or "no", followed by one sentence of reason: for yes, state the class transition or structural change (for example "low vegetation to building"); for no, state what stayed the same and the pseudo-change type (for example "roof recoloured, no change"). If the evidence is insufficient, say so.
